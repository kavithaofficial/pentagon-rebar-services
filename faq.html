Estimation FAQ & Technical Guidance.

Detailed answers regarding measurement standards, accepted plan formats, deliverable workbooks, turnaround times, and revision policies at PENTAGON REBAR SERVICES.

PENTAGON REBAR SERVICES specializes strictly in two preconstruction trades: 01 — Rebar Estimation (reinforcing steel takeoffs, itemized bar schedules, tonnage breakdowns) and 02 — Concrete Estimation (cast-in-place and precast cubic yardage takeoffs, pour phase segmentation, formwork SFCA). We do not provide detailing, shop drawings, structural engineering, or CAD/BIM modeling.
We accept standard PDF plan sets (vector and scanned raster), CAD drawings (.DWG, .DXF), and direct plan room invitations (Procore, PlanGrid, BuildingConnected, Google Drive, DropBox, OneDrive). For accurate takeoffs, we require structural foundation sheets, framing plans, structural general notes (SGN), and column/beam schedules.
All reinforcing steel takeoffs adhere to the CRSI (Concrete Reinforcing Steel Institute) Manual of Standard Practice and ACI 318 Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete. We adhere strictly to standard hook development rules, 90° and 135° seismic tie geometry, and project-specific lap splice schedules.
We apply the project's Structural General Notes lap splice tables (Class A and Class B splices). Where notes are silent, we apply CRSI standard splice lengths based on concrete compressive strength (f'c), bar yield strength (Grade 60/80), bar size, and coating type (epoxy vs. uncoated black steel). Splices are explicitly itemized rather than estimated with a blind percentage.
Standard deliverable packages include:
  • Microsoft Excel (.XLSX): Fully formula-transparent workbooks with summary sheets, tonnage by bar size, element breakouts, and customizable unit costs.
  • aSa-Compatible Takeoff Files: Structured data exports for fabricators using aSa estimating software.
  • Color-Coded Marked PDF Sets: Full-resolution drawings marked in Bluebeam Revu showing every measured member with layer separation.
Turnaround times depend on project gross square footage and structural complexity. Standard commercial projects (under 100,000 SF) typically require 3 to 5 business days. Expedited timelines (48-72 hours) are accommodated based on current team availability and bid due date deadlines.
We provide full pre-bid addenda support. When revisions or architectural/structural addenda are issued prior to your bid date, we compare the revised sheets against previous issues, quantify delta differences, and supply a revised summary indicating the exact tonnage and volume adjustments.
Yes. In our Concrete Estimation service, we calculate the exact Square Feet of Contact Area (SFCA) for edge forms, foundation wall faces, column forms, beam sides/soffits, and elevated deck plywood contact surfaces to give concrete subcontractors complete labor quantification.
Confidentiality is fundamental to preconstruction services. All drawings, takeoffs, contractor unit pricing, and project specifications are held in strict confidence. Documents are stored in encrypted environments and are never disclosed to third parties or competing bidders. We execute formal Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) on request.
