Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers to common questions about our rebar and concrete estimation services, required drawings, deliverables, and inquiry process.

Scope of Services

General & Company Scope

Pentagon Rebar Services specializes strictly in two preconstruction estimation services: 01 — Rebar Estimation (reinforcing steel quantity takeoffs, element breakdowns, and tonnage schedules) and 02 — Concrete Estimation (volumetric cubic yardage takeoffs, pour staging, and formwork SFCA). We do not provide detailing, shop drawings, structural engineering, or CAD/BIM modeling.
Our clients include general contractors bidding turnkey packages, rebar fabrication companies budgeting mill requirements, concrete subcontractors pricing placement and formwork, and preconstruction cost consulting teams needing an independent quantity takeoff.
Reinforcing Steel

Rebar Estimation Questions

We quantify all standard deformed bar sizes from #3 through #18 as specified on the drawings, categorized by ASTM grade (such as Grade 60, Grade 80, or epoxy-coated rebar where required). Each size is summarized by total linear length and converted to weight and short tons.
Yes. When required by project specifications or client scope, we calculate standard accessories including continuous high chairs, slab bolsters, beam bolsters, standees, tie wire allowances, and welded wire reinforcement (WWR/WWF).
We refer to the Structural General Notes (SGN) lap splice schedules provided by the structural engineer of record (e.g., Class B tension lap lengths or bar diameter multipliers). Splices are itemized systematically across continuous runs.
Cast-in-Place Concrete

Concrete Estimation Questions

Concrete volume is quantified element by element: continuous footings, isolated pads, grade beams, foundation walls, slab-on-grade, columns, elevated decks, and stairs. Each element is categorized by specified compressive strength (PSI) and pour stage.
Yes. When requested, we measure square footage of contact area (SFCA) for edge formwork, wall form surfaces, column forming, and elevated soffits to provide complete data for concrete placement labor and material planning.
Required Documents

Project Information & Drawings

We work primarily with vector or scanned PDF drawing sheets. You can share plans via direct cloud download links (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Procore, Box) or through our estimate request form.
When revisions or addenda bulletins are issued prior to your bid date, simply send the updated sheets. We compare the revisions against the previous issue and update the takeoff schedule to reflect the adjusted quantities.
Deliverables & Timing

Deliverables & Turnaround

Turnaround timing depends on the specific project scope, total sheet count, structural complexity, and your bid closing deadline. Upon receiving and reviewing your drawings, we confirm a mutually agreed delivery date before starting the takeoff.
Deliverables are provided in clean digital spreadsheets (Excel / CSV) with organized summary tabs, element-by-element quantity tables, weight totals, and notes detailing drawing references and scope assumptions.
Confidentiality is fundamental in preconstruction. All drawings, specifications, takeoffs, and project communications are held in strict confidence and are never shared with third parties or competing bidders. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) are accommodated on request.
