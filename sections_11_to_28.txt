Bar size

Quantity

Length

Weight

Tonnage

Structural element

Material information

Takeoff organization

Drawing references

BUT:

Do not invent the actual values.

Technical information belongs primarily on the relevant service pages or deliverable explanation sections.

Homepage = simplified visual communication.

Service page = deeper professional information.

This is the correct hierarchy.

27. SAMPLE DATA RULE

NEVER create fictional sample data and label it as Pentagon data.

Do not write fabricated examples such as:

“F-1 Footing — 48 bars — 3,004 lbs — 1.502 tons.”

Do not create fake project drawings.

Do not create fake estimate screenshots.

Do not create fake client documents.

If a demonstration is absolutely necessary:

It must be unmistakably labelled:

“ILLUSTRATIVE FORMAT — NOT A PENTAGON PROJECT”

But preferably, avoid invented technical numbers entirely until the company provides actual material.
