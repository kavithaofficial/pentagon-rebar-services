Company Overview

Independent Quantity Estimation Built on Blueprints

Pentagon Rebar Services provides professional rebar and concrete estimation services based on construction drawings and project information.

Accurate preconstruction estimating is the backbone of sound bidding and procurement. By conducting disciplined, element-by-element quantity takeoffs directly from structural and architectural sheets, we help contractors, fabricators, and project teams quantify reinforcement and concrete volume with transparency and clarity.

Specialized Scope

Core Estimation Services

We maintain a strictly focused service offering dedicated solely to preconstruction material quantification.

Service 01

Rebar Estimation

Comprehensive quantity takeoffs of reinforcing steel from structural drawings. We organize steel weight and linear lengths by bar size, grade, and structural element to support accurate bidding and mill procurement.

  • Element-by-element tonnage and weight quantification
  • Categorization by bar size (#3 through #18) and steel grade
  • Quantification of accessories, ties, bar supports, and dowels

Service 02

Concrete Estimation

Reliable volumetric calculations for all cast-in-place and structural concrete elements. Quantities are structured by pour phase, concrete strength specification, and element type for organized procurement and site planning.

  • Cubic yardage quantification partitioned by structural element
  • Breakdowns by design mix (PSI rating) and pour segmentation
  • Formwork contact area (SFCA) and embedded accessory tracking

Workflow

Our Estimation Process

A four-stage methodology designed for transparency from initial plan submission to final schedule delivery.

01

Project Information

You submit your project plans, drawings, and scope requirements through our estimate request portal or via direct file link.

02

Drawing & Scope Review

We review the structural sheets, notes, schedules, and addenda to confirm drawing completeness and align on project specifications.

03

Estimation & Quantification

Our team performs detailed measurement of reinforcement and concrete volumes, itemizing quantities element by element.

04

Schedule Delivery

You receive complete, structured takeoff schedules ready for bid preparation, subcontractor evaluation, or procurement planning.

Deliverable Architecture

How Our Takeoffs Are Structured

An overview of the categories, data fields, and element breakdowns typically included in an estimation deliverable.

Deliverable Framework

Standard Takeoff Organization Structure

Illustrative Structure Only
Category 01

Structural Element Partitioning

Quantities organized element by element: continuous & spread footings, foundation walls, grade beams, slab-on-grade, columns, elevated decks, and stair structures.

Category 02

Bar Specifications & Sizing

Reinforcing steel broken down by bar size (#3 through #18), steel grade (e.g. Grade 60, Grade 80), bar mark, cut length, lap splices, and hooks.

Category 03

Weight & Tonnage Summaries

Total calculated linear feet converted into unit weights (lbs) and tonnage totals, organized by structural element and overall project summary.

Category 04

Concrete Volume Calculations

Gross and net concrete volume quantified in cubic yards, categorized by pour stage, specified compressive strength (PSI), and element type.

Category 05

Accessories & Wire Mesh

Quantification of secondary reinforcing components including welded wire reinforcement (WWR/WWF), continuous bar chairs, standees, and tie accessories.

Category 06

Bid-Ready Schedule Formats

Clean, structured spreadsheet and document deliverables ready for direct import or integration into your estimating workflows and bid submissions.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about our estimation services, process, and deliverables.

Pentagon Rebar Services specializes exclusively in two preconstruction estimation services: 01 — Rebar Estimation (reinforcing steel tonnage, bar schedules, and element takeoffs) and 02 — Concrete Estimation (cubic yardage, pour segmentation, and element-by-element volume calculations).
To prepare a dependable quantity takeoff, we typically require structural drawing sheets (foundation plans, framing plans, sections, details, and schedules) along with relevant architectural drawings for dimensional verification and any issued addenda.
Turnaround timing depends on the specific project size, drawing complexity, number of structural sheets, and current queue. Upon reviewing your submitted plans and project scope, we confirm an agreed turnaround schedule before proceeding.
Deliverables are provided in organized digital formats, primarily structured spreadsheets (Excel / CSV) with element-by-element quantity breakdowns, weight calculations, bar classifications, and summary sheets ready for your bid workflows.
You can submit project details through our online Request an Estimate form, where you can share download links (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Procore) or provide project specifications directly.
