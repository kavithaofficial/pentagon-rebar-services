About Pentagon Rebar Services

Professional rebar and concrete quantity estimation services based on construction drawings and project documentation.

Who We Are

Independent Quantity Estimation for Preconstruction

Pentagon Rebar Services provides dedicated preconstruction quantity estimation services for general contractors, reinforcing steel fabricators, concrete subcontractors, and project estimating departments.

In modern construction, profitability and schedule adherence begin during the bid phase. Without a methodical takeoff, contractors face exposure to material shortfalls or uncompetitive pricing. Our role is to provide a clean, reliable, drawing-backed breakdown of steel tonnage and concrete volume before commitments are made.

We operate independently, offering an objective quantification service that can serve as a primary estimate for bidding or as a verification cross-check against internal numbers.

Strict Scope

What We Focus On

By concentrating our practice strictly on two core scopes, we preserve deep technical rigor in structural material takeoffs.

01

Rebar Estimation

Complete material takeoff of all deformed reinforcing steel, dowels, stirrups, ties, temperature mesh, and accessories. Organized by element, bar size (#3–#18), length, and tonnage for mill procurement and fabrication budgeting.

02

Concrete Estimation

Cubic yardage quantification for all cast-in-place structural elements. Partitioned by pour sequence, element geometry, compressive strength rating (PSI), and contact formwork area (SFCA) for accurate batch plant coordination.

Methodology

Our Estimation Approach

Our working methodology is built around clear drawing interpretation, structured classification, and transparent deliverables.

Drawing Thoroughness

We review structural sheets, general notes, schedule tables, and typical details to identify lap splice requirements, hook developments, and embedments.

Element Partitioning

Quantities are never lumped into arbitrary sums. Every item is mapped directly to its specific structural location: footing, wall, column, beam, or deck.

Clean Deliverable Sheets

Our spreadsheet schedules are structured for clarity, providing summary totals and detailed breakdowns ready for direct insertion into your bid packages.

Schedule Discipline

Bid deadlines are firm. We establish clear delivery targets before beginning work, ensuring you have the quantities needed in advance of bid closing.

Value to Preconstruction

Why Estimation Matters in Preconstruction

Structural concrete and reinforcing steel constitute one of the most cost-intensive scopes in building construction. Small variance in lap assumptions or element volumes can create significant budget gaps.

✓
Competitive Bid Protection: Avoid underbidding that erodes margin, and prevent overbidding that costs your team the contract.
✓
Procurement & Mill Planning: Accurate bar classifications allow fabricators to plan mill orders and stock inventories without emergency over-orders.
✓
Early Scope Clarification: Identifying drawing ambiguities or discrepancies between plans and schedules before field work starts.
