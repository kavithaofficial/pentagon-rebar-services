Privacy & Data Security Protocol.

PENTAGON REBAR SERVICES respects your proprietary engineering data. This policy outlines how we safeguard project blueprints, client RFQs, and estimation deliverables.

1. Purpose & Scope

PENTAGON REBAR SERVICES ("we," "our," "us") provides specialized preconstruction material quantification services, specifically Rebar Estimation and Concrete Estimation. This Privacy & Data Security Policy describes how we handle, protect, and process proprietary drawings, contract documents, project specifications, and personal contact details submitted to our firm.

2. Confidentiality of Architectural & Structural Drawings

We recognize that architectural, structural, civil, and geotechnical plan sets, along with contractor addenda and specifications, constitute valuable intellectual property and proprietary trade information.

  • Sole Usage Purpose: Project files are utilized solely to perform mathematical measurements, quantity takeoffs, and cost-modeling for the specific client who provided them.
  • No Third-Party Disclosure: We will never sell, lease, transfer, or disclose client plans, subcontractor bids, or takeoffs to competing bidders, general contractors, or outside vendors.
  • Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs): We routinely execute bilateral NDAs prior to accessing proprietary plan rooms or sensitive client files upon request.

3. Information We Collect

In the course of providing preconstruction estimates, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Name, corporate email address, direct telephone number, company identity, and business role (General Contractor, Fabricator, Subcontractor).
  • Project Data: Project title, geographic location, bid date deadlines, gross floor area parameters, and drawing download links.
  • Technical Correspondence: Clarification notes regarding structural details, addenda bulletins, and specific scope exclusions.

4. Digital Security & Storage Controls

All digital drawings, takeoff worksheets, and communications are stored on secure servers with restricted access controls. Access to project files is limited exclusively to estimators actively assigned to your takeoff. Takeoff files are retained only for the duration required to support bid negotiations, addenda deltas, and client post-bid queries, after which they are archived under encrypted protocols.

5. Inquiries & Data Rights

Clients may request verification, modification, or complete deletion of their contact records and archived project files at any time by contacting our preconstruction administration desk at rfq@pentagonrebarservices.com.
