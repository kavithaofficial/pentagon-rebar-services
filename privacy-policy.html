Privacy Policy

How Pentagon Rebar Services collects, handles, and protects client project drawings, preconstruction specifications, and commercial inquiries.

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At Pentagon Rebar Services, we recognize that preconstruction structural drawings, addenda bulletins, and trade bidding documents represent proprietary commercial information. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of all project materials submitted through our website or direct communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly by clients when requesting an estimate or contacting our team, including:

  • Contact details: Name, company name, corporate email address, and telephone number.
  • Project data: Project title, general location, bid due date, and scope notes.
  • Drawing documents: Structural blueprints, architectural sheets, specifications, and cloud plan room download links.

3. Use of Project Documents

Submitted drawings, schedules, and specifications are utilized exclusively for the purpose of evaluating project scope, calculating rebar and concrete quantity takeoffs, and preparing estimation deliverables. We do not sell, license, or distribute project documents to third parties or competing contractors.

4. Document Storage & Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect drawing files and commercial correspondence against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Access to project files is restricted strictly to estimators assigned to the project review.

5. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

We routinely execute mutual or client-provided Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to receiving sensitive preconstruction drawings or bidding documentation. Please contact us directly if your organization requires an executed NDA before transmitting drawing links.

6. Inquiries Regarding Privacy

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our document protection protocols, please contact our preconstruction desk at drawings@pentagonrebar.com.
