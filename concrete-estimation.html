Concrete Estimation Services

Volumetric cubic yardage calculations, element-by-element takeoffs, and formwork contact area (SFCA) derived directly from structural plans.

Scope Definition

What is Concrete Estimation?

Concrete estimation is the systematic quantification of all cast-in-place and structural concrete volumes required for a project, based on issued structural and architectural drawings.

Rather than using basic footprint multipliers, a comprehensive concrete takeoff calculates the specific three-dimensional volume of each foundation member, wall, column, slab, and beam.

Volumes are converted into cubic yards (CY) and categorized by pour stage, specified compressive strength (PSI), and related formwork contact area (SFCA) to facilitate procurement and job-site coordination.

Practical Value

Why Concrete Estimation Matters

How accurate volume quantification protects field budgets, material schedules, and subcontractor relations.

Batch Plant Coordination

Having exact cubic yardage by mix design allows concrete contractors to schedule ready-mix delivery trucks and manage batch plant commitments smoothly.

Formwork Planning

Quantifying square feet of contact area (SFCA) alongside cubic yardage enables accurate formwork lumber, modular panel, and shoring rental budgeting.

Minimizing Material Waste

Avoid costly short-load charges or excess concrete waste by establishing clear volumetric benchmarks for each distinct pour phase.

Bid Package Validation

General contractors use our independent takeoffs to review subcontractor proposals, confirming that concrete trade quotes reflect complete plan scope.

Drawing Input

What Information is Needed?

To prepare a comprehensive concrete quantity takeoff, our team works with typical preconstruction plan sets:

  • Structural Foundation & Framing Plans: Member dimensions, slab depths, grade beam layouts, and column cross-sections.
  • Concrete Specifications & Notes: Mix requirements, minimum 28-day compressive strengths (e.g. 3,000 PSI, 4,000 PSI, 5,000 PSI), and curing requirements.
  • Architectural Elevation & Section Sheets: Finished floor elevations, step locations, depression details, and perimeter grade transitions.
  • Geotechnical / Civil Sheets (if available): For slab sub-base, vapor retarder details, or specific earth-formed footing requirements.
Scope Details

What Can Be Estimated?

Our concrete takeoffs cover substructure, superstructure, and secondary placement scopes.

Element 01

Foundation Substructure

Continuous wall footings, spread/isolated footings, grade beams, pile caps, caisson caps, and elevator pit slabs with net cubic yardage calculations.

Element 02

Slab-on-Grade (SOG)

Interior slabs, thickened slab edges, turned-down footings, equipment pads, and vapor barrier square footage calculations.

Element 03

Walls & Columns

Basement walls, foundation stem walls, shear walls, retaining walls, piers, and columns quantified by height, thickness, and mix strength.

Element 04

Elevated Decks & Beams

Cast-in-place elevated slabs, slab on metal deck (SOMD), post-tensioned decks, structural drop panels, and concrete transfer girders.

Element 05

Formwork Contact Area (SFCA)

Square footage of contact area measured for footing edge forms, wall forms (both faces), column forms, slab edges, and soffit shoring.

Element 06

Specialty Items & Joints

Quantification of construction joint keys, expansion joint fillers, waterstops, curing compounds, and concrete stair pan infill.
