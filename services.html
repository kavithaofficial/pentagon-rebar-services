Services Overview

Pentagon Rebar Services specializes strictly in two core preconstruction offerings: Rebar Estimation and Concrete Estimation.

Service 01

Rebar Estimation

A complete takeoff of all reinforcing steel required by project blueprints. We translate structural drawings into organized steel weight, linear length, and bar schedules.

Why It Matters: Steel is a volatile, high-value material. Accurate tonnage prevents material under-purchasing and provides a solid basis for mill order planning.
What You Provide: Structural foundation plans, framing sheets, column/beam schedules, structural general notes, and issued addenda.
What You Receive: Structured spreadsheet schedules with element breakdowns, bar sizes (#3–#18), linear lengths, calculated weights (lbs/tons), and accessories.

Service 02

Concrete Estimation

Disciplined cubic yardage takeoffs for cast-in-place and structural concrete elements. We calculate volumes by structural element, pour sequence, and design strength.

Why It Matters: Concrete orders require precise volume staging to coordinate batch plants, pump trucks, and pouring crews without costly site downtime.
What You Provide: Structural foundation plans, framing plans, slab elevations, concrete mix notes/specifications, and architectural site sections.
What You Receive: Net and gross cubic yardage schedules partitioned by element, PSI mix rating, pour phase, and formwork contact area (SFCA).
