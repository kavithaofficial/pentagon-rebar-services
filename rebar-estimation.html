Service 01

Rebar Estimation Services

Accurate reinforcing steel takeoffs derived directly from structural drawings to support bidding, budgeting, and mill procurement.

Scope Definition

What is Rebar Estimation?

Rebar estimation is the process of measuring, quantifying, and organizing every piece of reinforcing steel indicated on structural drawings before construction begins.

Rather than relying on rough square-footage or cubic-yard approximations, a dedicated rebar estimate extracts exact linear dimensions, bar classifications, lap splice allowances, hook geometry, and accessory counts from the structural plans.

The resulting takeoff delivers total calculated weights and tonnages organized by member, giving estimators and project managers the precise data needed to price steel competitively.

Practical Value

Why Rebar Estimation Matters

How methodical reinforcement takeoffs protect project budgets and support procurement operations.

Material Cost Planning

Reinforcing steel prices fluctuate. Having an exact tonnage breakdown allows your team to apply current mill or distributor pricing with confidence.

Procurement Support

Classifying steel by bar size (#3 through #18) enables fabricators to plan inventory stock and order lead times well in advance of pour dates.

Risk Reduction

Eliminates assumptions on lap lengths, starter dowels, and corner bar schedules, preventing unexpected field change orders or tonnage shortfalls.

Subcontractor Alignment

General contractors use our clean element schedules to verify sub bids, ensuring all trade partners are quoting the exact same scope of steel.

Drawing Input

What Information is Needed?

To produce a comprehensive rebar takeoff, our estimators work with the project documents issued for bidding or construction:

  • Structural Drawing Set: Foundation plans, column schedules, framing layouts, beam details, and wall sections.
  • Structural General Notes: Specifications governing steel grade, lap splice classes, cover clearances, and hook requirements.
  • Architectural Drawings (if applicable): Floor plans and wall sections for verifying boundary dimensions and openings.
  • Issued Addenda: Any revision bulletins or addenda sheets impacting reinforcement schedules.
Deliverable Content

What the Estimate Organizes

Our takeoffs break down reinforcement by structural category, bar sizing, and material classifications.

Scope 01

Structural Element Partitioning

Organized by continuous footings, isolated pads, grade beams, foundation walls, slab-on-grade, columns, elevated decks, and stair reinforcement.

Scope 02

Bar Sizes & Specifications

Individual breakdowns for each bar size from #3 up to #18, classified by ASTM grade (Grade 60, Grade 80, epoxy-coated where specified).

Scope 03

Quantity & Linear Lengths

Itemized count of bars, individual cut lengths, hook lengths, lap splice allowances, and total linear feet per element.

Scope 04

Weight & Tonnage Totals

Converted into standard unit weights (lbs) and short tons per element, per floor level, and summarized for total project tonnage.

Scope 05

Dowels & Starter Bars

Detailed tracking of starter dowels connecting footings to walls, piers to columns, and slab-to-wall tie-ins.

Scope 06

Accessories & Supports

Quantification of continuous high chairs, slab bolsters, beam bolsters, tie wire, and welded wire reinforcement (WWR/WWF).
