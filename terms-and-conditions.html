Service Terms & Conditions.

Standard B2B terms governing preconstruction material estimation, quantity takeoffs, client document obligations, and scope boundaries.

1. Agreement & Scope of Services

By commissioning estimation services from PENTAGON REBAR SERVICES ("Pentagon"), the client ("Client") agrees to these Terms and Conditions.

Pentagon provides strictly preconstruction quantity takeoffs and material schedules for:

  • 01 — Rebar Estimation: Measuring reinforcing steel weights, bar schedules, splices, and support accessories.
  • 02 — Concrete Estimation: Volumetric cubic yardage calculations, element segmentation, and formwork contact areas (SFCA).

2. Professional Estimation Boundary (Not Engineer of Record)

Pentagon operates strictly as a preconstruction material estimator. Pentagon does not act as the licensed structural Engineer of Record (EOR), architect, or professional engineer. Our takeoffs and deliverables are prepared solely for contractor bidding, cost modeling, and material procurement planning based upon drawings supplied by the Client. We do not provide structural design certifications, rebar fabrication shop drawings, or structural stability calculations.

3. Client Documentation & Accuracy of Supplied Plans

The accuracy of any quantity takeoff is contingent upon the completeness and clarity of the structural and architectural drawings supplied by the Client. The Client is responsible for providing complete drawing sets, Structural General Notes (SGN), applicable addenda, and specification books. Pentagon is not liable for material discrepancies resulting from missing drawing sheets, unissued addenda, or illegible scanned blueprints.

4. Measurement Standards & Ambiguities

Unless otherwise instructed in writing by the Client, reinforcing steel is quantified according to the CRSI (Concrete Reinforcing Steel Institute) Manual of Standard Practice and ACI 318 guidelines. Where drawing notes are ambiguous or conflicting, Pentagon estimators apply standard industry conventions and explicitly log the assumption within the takeoff deliverable for client review.

5. Addenda & Revision Requests

When project addenda, bulletins, or revised sheets are issued subsequent to quotation or takeoff commencement, Pentagon will review the revisions and provide an addendum delta estimate based on the scope of changes and remaining bid timeline.

6. Deliverable Ownership & Licensing

Upon full settlement of agreed service fees, the Client is granted an unrestricted, perpetual license to use the delivered Excel workbooks, markup PDFs, and takeoff data for internal bidding, project budgeting, and subcontractor negotiations. Pentagon retains ownership of proprietary calculation algorithms and template formats.

7. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Pentagon's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to any estimation project shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client for that specific estimation assignment. Pentagon shall not be liable for consequential, incidental, indirect, or lost profit damages arising from contractor bid submissions.

PENTAGON REBAR SERVICES is a specialized preconstruction quantity takeoff company delivering engineered rebar and concrete estimation solutions for contractors and fabricators.
