Terms & Conditions

Terms governing preconstruction estimation services, drawing interpretation scope, and quantity takeoff deliverables.

1. Service Definition & Scope

Pentagon Rebar Services provides preconstruction material quantity estimation services strictly limited to: (a) Rebar Estimation and (b) Concrete Estimation. Our deliverables consist of quantity takeoffs, linear lengths, calculated weights, short tonnages, and cubic yardage schedules derived from contract drawings provided by the client.

Pentagon Rebar Services does not provide structural engineering, architectural design, rebar detailing, fabrication placement drawings, or construction management services. Our takeoffs do not replace the professional judgment or sealed documents of the engineer of record.

2. Reliance on Client Drawings

All quantity estimations are generated directly from the drawings, schedules, addenda, and specifications supplied by the client. The client is responsible for providing complete, current, and legible drawing sets. Any changes, addenda, or scope adjustments issued after commencement may require an updated scope evaluation and revised timeline.

3. Deliverable Purpose & Limitation of Liability

Estimation schedules and takeoffs provided by Pentagon Rebar Services are prepared to support preconstruction budgeting, contractor bid preparation, and material procurement planning. Because field conditions, installation methods, and fabrication tolerances vary, the client and its contractor partners remain responsible for final bid pricing, field verifications, and trade subcontracts.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Pentagon Rebar Services shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from the use of estimation data in bidding or procurement.

4. Proprietary Information & Confidentiality

All proprietary drawings, addenda, unit rates, and bid parameters shared with Pentagon Rebar Services are treated with strict confidentiality. Deliverables generated for a specific project belong to the client upon receipt and payment.

5. Amendments

We may update these terms periodically to reflect operational adjustments. Continued engagement of our estimation services following updates constitutes acceptance of revised terms.

6. Contact Inquiries

For any inquiries regarding these terms, please contact us at drawings@pentagonrebar.com.
