Structural Typologies & Takeoff Profiles.

Review the structural typologies, foundation systems, and element complexities handled by PENTAGON REBAR SERVICES in commercial, residential, industrial, and civil construction.

Structural Experience Framework

Estimation Capabilities by Sector.

Every building classification presents unique reinforcing congestion, splice standards, and concrete pour staging. We tailor our takeoff breakdowns to the exact structural framing system.

Commercial Office & Retail

Multi-tier continuous foundations, heavy spread footings, multi-story shear wall elevator cores, cast-in-place columns, and post-tensioned mild steel framing.

Key Focus: Seismic column tie confinement & core wall opening trims
Mat Slabs Core Walls Mild Steel PT

Multi-Family Residential Podiums

Type I concrete podium slabs supporting multi-level wood or light-gauge steel framing. Subterranean parking garages, retaining walls, and PT transfer decks.

Key Focus: Heavy drop panel punching shear steel & perimeter stem walls
Podium Slabs Drop Panels Retaining Walls

Industrial Warehouses & Tilt-Up

High-tolerance Slab-on-Grade (FF/FL flat floors), perimeter tilt-up panels, continuous footing keys, loading dock leveler pits, and heavy machinery foundations.

Key Focus: Contraction dowel baskets, WWF wire mesh & panel chord steel
Tilt-Up Panels Dowel Baskets Equipment Pads

Civil & Highway Infrastructure

Bridge abutments, pier caps, crash barriers, precast girder bearings, cast-in-place box culverts, retaining structures, and heavy concrete sound barrier walls.

Key Focus: Epoxy-coated bar allowances & mass concrete pour staging
Bridge Abutments Box Culverts Mass Concrete

Water Treatment & Environmental

Wastewater treatment clarifiers, aeration basins, pump wet wells, water containment tanks, and hazardous material secondary containment dykes.

Key Focus: Waterstop joints, chemical-resistant rebar & dense crack-control steel
Tank Basins PVC Waterstops High-Density Mats

Healthcare & Institutional

Hospital expansions, university research centers, vibration-isolated medical equipment slabs, and radiation vault high-density shielding walls.

Key Focus: High-density bar cages & structural coordinate verification
Radiation Vaults Vibration Control Drilled Shafts
