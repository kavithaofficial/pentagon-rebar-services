Construction Applications

Preconstruction rebar and concrete quantity estimation across diverse structural systems, building typologies, and foundation complexities.

Building Typologies

Estimation Scope by Sector

Every structural typology introduces specific reinforcement configurations and concrete pour considerations. We structure takeoffs to align with each sector's requirements.

Commercial & Mixed-Use

Office developments, retail plazas, hotel structures, and multi-use facilities featuring deep foundation footings, shear wall elevator cores, cast-in-place columns, and multi-level elevated slabs.

Key Elements: Continuous Footings • Columns • Shear Walls • Elevated Slabs

Multi-Family Residential Podiums

Concrete podium slabs supporting wood or light-gauge framed apartments and condominiums. Encompasses subterranean parking garages, retaining walls, post-tensioned mild steel, and perimeter footings.

Key Elements: Podium Slabs • Retaining Walls • Grade Beams • Drop Panels

Industrial & Logistics

Distribution centers, manufacturing warehouses, and cold storage facilities with large-acreage slab-on-grade, tilt-up panel framing, heavy dowel baskets, and heavy equipment foundation pads.

Key Elements: Heavy SOG • Tilt-Up Panels • Dowel Baskets • Equipment Pads

Civil & Infrastructure

Bridge abutments, pier caps, box culverts, retaining walls, drainage channels, and highway barrier concrete. Focus on epoxy-coated rebar allowances and mass concrete pour coordination.

Key Elements: Bridge Abutments • Box Culverts • Mass Pours • Barrier Rails

Institutional & Healthcare

Hospitals, educational facilities, and municipal buildings characterized by stringent structural specifications, heavy equipment vibrations, and multi-phase preconstruction bidding schedules.

Key Elements: Radiation Vaults • Mat Foundations • Deep Piers • Stair Towers

Water & Environmental

Water reservoirs, aeration basins, pump wet wells, wastewater clarifiers, and secondary containment structures requiring waterstop joint quantification and dense crack-control reinforcement.

Key Elements: Tank Basins • Waterstop Joints • Sump Pits • Thickened Slabs
